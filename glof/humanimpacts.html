Human Impact Overview

Glacial Lake Outburst Floods (GLOFs) in Pakistan have devastating effects on local communities, infrastructure, and livelihoods. Regions such as Gilgit-Baltistan and Chitral are particularly vulnerable due to their proximity to glaciers. These floods often lead to loss of life, displacement, destruction of farmland, and damage to critical infrastructure like roads and bridges.

Climate change has accelerated glacier melt, increasing the risk of GLOFs. Communities living near glacial lakes face rising challenges, including access to resources and relocation in response to floods. Strategies to mitigate these impacts include early warning systems, improved infrastructure, and awareness campaigns.
